What is a home warranty?
A service contract that covers the repair or replacement of important appliances and system components that break down over time. It protects your home, your budget and your time.
What does a home warranty cover?
A typical plan covers heating, plumbing, electrical, water heaters, kitchen appliances and more. A Total Plan adds air conditioning, refrigerator, washer and dryer. Optional add-ons cover items like a pool, well pump, sump pump, septic system or roof leaks.
Is it the same as homeowners insurance?
No. Insurance covers sudden damage like fire or storms. A home warranty covers breakdowns from normal wear and tear, which are not covered under an insurance policy.
How do I request service?
Visit our online account center or call us at (917) 495-4300. Our service center is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. We connect you with qualified repair professionals in your area.
What does a home warranty cost?
It varies by your place of residence, the level of protection you want, the size of your dwelling and the type of home. Get a quick quote online to see your price. There are no hidden fees.
What do I pay when something breaks?
Other than a trade call fee and any deductibles, the cost of covered repairs is handled under your plan. When the service provider arrives, there is nothing more you need to do.
What if an item can't be repaired?
If a covered item cannot be repaired, your plan will replace it. Prime Shield reserves the right to offer cash back in lieu of repair or replacement in the amount of its actual cost. See the user agreement.
Who does the repairs?
Service contractors in our network are fully insured and licensed before undergoing our vetting process. You never have to go out and look for the right professional.
How does the first month free work?
Purchase any single-payment home warranty plan and the first month is on us.* See the user agreement for details.
